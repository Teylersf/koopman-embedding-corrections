2. **Changes to practical numerical prescriptions.** New Section 7 separates the quantitative consequences. For the same nonlinear shear with width 0.01 and step 0.001, the printed normalized geometric quantity is about 1.0000098691 while the increment statistic is about 0.0044399602. Other constraints may be stricter; both accept the small width used in the source's plotted example. We do not infer failure of that plotted orbit.
