## Version 1.1.0

Taylor Kalin's revised correction and reproducibility study of arXiv:2609.28999v2.

- Adds a width-uniform complete-flow increment bound, including packet width proportional to time step.
- Gives a smooth probability-density realization of the same-fringe/different-mean example under a conservative Liouville flow.
- Identifies the circular-moment identity as standard directional statistics, with a supporting reference.
- Quantifies changes to the geometric test, sampling budgets and nominal packet-width calibration.
- Archives six short, attributed source excerpts with original TeX line numbers, checksums and an independent verifier.
- Compares the failing smaller appendix covariance bound with the larger main-text constant that holds in the same example.
- Adds a seventh deterministic verification group; all seven pass. Execution code is pinned to commit 1f006c4d0acae13b2ccee9e485cb31288c2cdc0d.

The paper preserves the valid nonlinear cloud-to-trajectory discrepancy and does not establish a universal replacement threshold or a quantum speedup. Taylor Kalin is the canonical author. The study is self-funded; AI assistance is disclosed in the methods. Venue decisions and public copies are tracked separately in submissions/publication-status.json.
